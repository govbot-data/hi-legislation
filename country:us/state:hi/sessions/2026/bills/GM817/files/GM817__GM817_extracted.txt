Aloha Senate President Kouchi:
I am pleased to inform you that pursuant to section 17-3(b)(3), Hawaii Revised
Statutes, I hereby appoint Ms. Danielle M. Bass to temporarily serve as the senator
from Senate District 18 (Waipi'o, Crestview, Royal Kunia, a portion of Mililani, a portion
of Waipahu, and a portion of Akupu). Ms. Bass shall begin her service immediately.
This appointment will expire on November 3, 2026, and fills the vacancy created by the
recent retirement of Senator Michelle N. Kidani, who served in the legislature for 18
years.
